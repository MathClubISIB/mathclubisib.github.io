## COVID-19 period

During the COVID-19 disruptions, the Math Club continued its mathematical activities and community engagement entirely online.

The [UDGRP](../activities/udgrp/) had only a Summer edition during this period. It was initiated by Snehinh Sen and began in [Summer 2021](../activities/udgrp/summer_2021/).
