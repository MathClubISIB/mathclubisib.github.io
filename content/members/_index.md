> The Math Club was established in the year 2018 by the students of ISI Bangalore. An initiative that started to organize just student talks has now grown into a properly structured student-run organization that organizes talks by professors and students alike, brings forth professors and alumni for [meet and greet sessions](../activities/meet_n_greet/), and also organizes [UDGRPs](../activities/udgrp/) that aims to improve mathematical communication and introduce the plethora of topics that mathematics subsumes. The [UDGRP initiative](../activities/udgrp/summer_2021/) was started by Snehinh Sen (B. Math 2022) in 2021, and was extended into a [winter edition](../activities/udgrp/winter_2022/) by Soumya Dasgupta (B. Math 2024) in 2022. The [meet and greet sessions](../activities/meet_n_greet/mng_2021/) started in 2021. Though now the Math Club is self-sufficient, we would like to thank the professors of ISI Bangalore, without whose patronage the Math Club could not have been as organized as it is today.
>
> Written by Hrishik Koley (B. Math, 2025)
