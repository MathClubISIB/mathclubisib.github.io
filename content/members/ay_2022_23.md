## Rebuilding the club

After the COVID-19 disruptions, these members rebuilt the Math Club's activities from scratch. They revived offline activities, recorded almost all the talks, and continued the remote online talks established during the COVID-19 period, along with Meet & Greet sessions.

The Winter edition of [UDGRP](../activities/udgrp/) initiated by Soumya Dasgupta also began in [Winter 2022](../activities/udgrp/winter_2022/).
