The Math Club was founded in 2018 by students of ISI Bangalore. During its early years, it maintained a decent WordPress website.

The COVID-19 pandemic disrupted in-person campus activities during the 2020-21 period. The Math Club continued to organize activities online through Zoom and Google Meet.

The following are the members who contributed to the Math Club's activities during the 2018-21 period.
